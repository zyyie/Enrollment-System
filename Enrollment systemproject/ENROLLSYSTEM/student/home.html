General Guidelines for Senior High School Enrollment — Universal Access to Quality Education

  1. All Grade 11 and Grade 12 students who meet admission requirements are eligible for the Senior High School Voucher Program, unless they fall under ineligible categories as defined by the Department of Education.
  2. Students must maintain good scholastic standing. Students who incur scholastic delinquency (failing or dropping subjects) shall be subject to the following actions based on their subject load in the previous semester.

* A student who receives a Warning for two consecutive semesters shall have the voucher grant forfeited for the following semester.

  1. Students who stay beyond the allowed number of years for their Senior High School program shall pay for the remaining years of stay.
  2. Students intending to file for a leave of absence must follow the procedures in the Student Handbook. Authorized leaves allow the student to return and still enjoy the voucher.
  3. Existing admission, retention, and graduation rules of the school shall apply. In case of inconsistency, the Student Handbook shall prevail.
